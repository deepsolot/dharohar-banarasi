Our Story

Where Every Thread
Tells a Story

Founded in 1979 by the Choudhary family in the heart of Varanasi, Dharohar Banarasi is more than a brand — it is a living tradition. For over 45 years, we have worked directly with master weavers who have inherited this art across generations.

Each saree takes 15 days to 6 months to craft, with weavers meticulously interlacing gold and silver zari threads into silk on traditional handlooms. The result is wearable art that carries the soul of Banaras.
